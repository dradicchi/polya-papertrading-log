# Post-Mortem PM-003 — 2026-06-16

**The "grinding" variant: a large but slow rally (+9% over six days) that
neither the early-close mechanism nor the tail hedge protected against, yet
the book absorbed while staying P&L-positive.**

---

## Summary

Between 2026-06-10 and 2026-06-16, BTC rose from approximately 60,900 to
66,400 USD — a **+9% move spread over six days (~1.5% per day)** that did
not reverse. The near-the-money daily short-CALL book carried a core
realized drawdown of about **−0.14 BTC** (1 contract per signal, paper)
over 13–16 Jun, driven entirely by contracts settling in-the-money at the
scheduled 08:00 UTC daily expiration.

Two things distinguish PM-003 from the prior tail event (PM-001):

1. **The damage came from magnitude delivered slowly, not from speed.** The
   per-day velocity stayed *below* the threshold at which a short call
   spread becomes useful, so the **tail hedge paid nothing** — 0 payouts
   across 41 hedged trades.
2. **A deeper out-of-the-money configuration was structurally immune**
   (≈ −0.01 BTC): the move's velocity stayed below its break-even, so its
   strikes were never reached.

No stopping rule was triggered. **Cumulative P&L stayed positive throughout**
the window, and the book began regenerating on 16 Jun — documented separately
in [`analysis/regeneration-pm-cycle-2026-06.md`](../analysis/regeneration-pm-cycle-2026-06.md).
A residual in-the-money tail was still settling at the time of writing; the
figures below are provisional in that respect.

## What happened

### Spot trajectory

| Date | BTC (USD) | Δ |
|---|---|---|
| Jun 10 | 60,900 | (trough) |
| Jun 11 | 63,000 | +3.4% |
| Jun 12 | 63,600 | +1.0% |
| Jun 13 | 64,400 | +1.2% |
| Jun 14 | 64,500 | +0.2% |
| Jun 15 | 66,300 | +2.8% |
| Jun 16 | 66,400 | +0.1% |

A monotonic climb — no single-day flash, no intra-move reversal — totalling
+9% in six days.

### Trade outcomes (core window 13–16 Jun)

- **Near-ATM book:** realized ≈ −0.14 BTC; **100% of the losers exited via
  the terminal expiry path** (settled in-the-money), not via the normal
  early close. The early-close mechanism does not fire on a rising market —
  the option premium does not fall — so positions stay exposed to the
  settlement.
- **Deep-OTM book:** ≈ −0.01 BTC. Effectively immune.
- **Tail-hedged book (short call spread, ~5% OTM long leg):** the short
  legs were byte-identical to the unhedged book; the long legs paid **0 of
  41**, costing ~0.05 BTC in premium. Net result strictly worse than
  unhedged over this event.

## Mechanism (consistent with vol-selling literature)

A short out-of-the-money CALL is short the underlying's upside. Whether a
position settles in-the-money is governed not by the total size of a rally
but by the **velocity** of the move relative to the position's break-even —
the realized-vs-implied daily move that defines the gamma/theta crossover
for a short-gamma book.

PM-003 sat in the gap between two defences:

- **Too slow for the tail hedge.** A fixed-strike spread only pays when the
  underlying crosses its strike gap *within the life of the contract*. A
  +9% move delivered at ~1.5%/day never moves a single short-dated contract
  far enough; the hedge is structurally a **velocity** instrument, not a
  **magnitude** one. This is the same conclusion reached in earlier hedge
  research and is now confirmed live.
- **Fast enough for the near-ATM book.** Its break-even is low, so a
  sustained ~1.5%/day grind is sufficient to push successive contracts
  in-the-money — but the deeper out-of-the-money book's higher break-even
  was not reached.

This is the literature's distinction between *flash* and *grind* events for
short-vol portfolios (Bondarenko 2014 on the asymmetry of short-vol payoffs;
Bakshi–Kapadia 2003 on the volatility risk premium). PM-001 was a fast,
near-terminal move; PM-003 is its slow-magnitude counterpart.

## What this confirms

- The terminal-settlement channel identified in PM-001 is the dominant loss
  path for the near-ATM configuration in a sustained rally.
- The tail hedge is **not** an expected-value-positive instrument and does
  **not** cover the grind regime. Its role, if any, is catastrophe
  insurance against a *fast* extreme move — a separate product decision, not
  a return enhancement. PM-003 is the second live confirmation (after a
  prior reversal-type event) that the hedge is net-negative in non-extreme
  conditions.
- Moneyness is the structural defence against the grind: the deeper
  out-of-the-money book absorbs it at the cost of materially lower return.

## What this does *not* change

No methodology change is made on the basis of this event. The primary
configuration remains the near-ATM book; its defence against this class of
event is **regeneration between events**, not avoidance — and that defence
holds for *sparse* adverse events (the regime observed) while remaining
under test for *sustained* rallies. The deeper out-of-the-money variant
remains a paper reference; the tail hedge remains under live evaluation as a
catastrophe-only overlay.
