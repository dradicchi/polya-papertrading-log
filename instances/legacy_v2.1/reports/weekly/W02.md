## ⚠ Methodology disclosure — window 2026-04-13 to 2026-04-15

1. **Moneyness filters missing** (B183/B184): 4 ITM contracts (x≥1.0) and 1 monthly at x=0.283 (outside training range) were entered.
2. **Cross-universe scoring** (B186): 25 of the first 87 pre-fix trades were scored with the wrong horizon model (16 daily were week, 5 weekly were month, 4 weekly were day).
3. **Operational filter mismatches** (B188): X_MAX weekly, T_FILTER monthly, EXPIRY_GUARD monthly+weekly did not match the backtest.

**Impact on realized PnL: zero.** All trades closed pre-fix are daily × `day` (correct match). The 25 open cross-universe positions (2.85 BTC IM) were left untouched — alpha-exit and expiry-guard handle them. Trades after 2026-04-15 22:00 UTC operate under the canonical universe.
