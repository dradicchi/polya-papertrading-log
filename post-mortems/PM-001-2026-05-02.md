# Post-Mortem PM-001 — 2026-05-02

**First adverse tail event in paper trading v2 since operational
refinements activated 2026-04-22.**

---

## Summary

A directional move in BTC of approximately +3.6% over 52 hours produced
five short CALL contracts settling in-the-money on 2026-05-02 at the
scheduled 08:00 UTC daily expiration. Aggregate net P&L impact:
**−0.063 BTC** over a 17-hour observation window (snapshot diff
2026-05-01 21:49 UTC → 2026-05-02 14:24 UTC). Cumulative-since-go-live
daily-horizon Sharpe: **+8.80 → +5.54**. Combined Sharpe: **+8.86 → +6.16**.

No stopping rule was triggered. No methodology change is being made in
response to this single event. This document records what happened, what
it confirms, and what it does *not* change.

### Trade outcomes (closed in window 2026-05-01 21:49 → 2026-05-02 14:24 UTC)

Nine daily contracts closed in the window. Five settled with adverse
outcomes; four marginal positives. All affected contracts shared the
2026-05-02 08:00 UTC expiration (`BTC-2MAY26-*-C`).

The five affected contracts settled with closing-price-to-entry-price
multipliers ranging from 1.13× to 3.13×, all closed via scheduled
expiration handling. Aggregate net P&L: −0.063 BTC. The four positives
either exited via early-exit before the move materialized, or had
sufficient distance from the spot at entry to remain marginal at
settlement. Aggregate net P&L of positives: +0.003 BTC. Net batch:
**−0.061 BTC**.

### Forward exposure (snapshot 2026-05-02 14:24 UTC)

Three daily contracts open at that snapshot, scheduled for 2026-05-03
expiration, were already in-the-money against the prevailing spot.
Estimated incremental impact if BTC remains above the relevant strikes
through tomorrow's settlement: approximately **−$0.038 BTC**. This is
not a methodology change — it is the natural unwinding of positions
opened before the directional move.

## Mechanism (consistent with vol-selling literature)

The event is a textbook realization of the **skew/jump risk premium**
that a short-OTM-CALL writer carries by design:

- In equity markets, a short put writer collects premium in approximately
  95% of months and pays a large adverse outcome in approximately 5%
  (Bondarenko 2014, *"Why Are Put Options So Expensive?"*). The
  expected return is positive over time precisely because the writer
  is being compensated for carrying the tail.
- In BTC options, the symmetric position is a short OTM CALL writer.
  The tail event for that position is a directional move *up*, not
  down. The mechanism is structurally identical; only the direction
  of the adverse move differs.
- Polya's paper-trading strategy is **short OTM CALL** on BTC,
  publicly stated in `README.md`. This is the first material
  realization of the expected adverse direction since the operational
  refinements documented in `DISCLOSURES.md` sessions 59-61 became
  active on 2026-04-22.

The asymmetric P&L profile — many small positive outcomes plus
infrequent material adverse outcomes of magnitude several times the
average gain — is a known structural property of vol-selling. It is
not a model failure. The combined-since-go-live cumulative metrics
(Sharpe, Sortino, win rate) absorb this event without invalidating
the underlying approach.

## What this confirms

1. **The trade-off documented in session 61 (DISCLOSURES.md) is real
   in live execution**, not only retrospective. The intra-cycle
   ranking change applied to the daily horizon prioritizes contracts
   nearer to the spot, which captures higher absolute premium per unit
   of margin in stable conditions, and amplifies the realized loss in
   adverse directional moves. This is the trade-off as understood at
   the time of the change; this event makes it visible in P&L.

2. **The early-exit asymmetry** — the alpha-exit rule fires only on
   favorable price decay, not on adverse price increase — means that
   contracts in adverse positions remain exposed until scheduled
   settlement. The magnitude of the adverse outcome at settlement is
   therefore a function of the spot's distance from the strike at
   that moment, with no intermediate stop. This is documented
   structural behavior, not a discovery.

3. **The cumulative-since-go-live Sharpe of +19 measured over the 9
   trading days from 2026-04-22 to 2026-04-30** was an artifact of a
   clean window prior to any adverse directional move. That number is
   not a sustainable steady-state expectation. The honest forward
   expectation, calibrated to peer vol-fund benchmarks, is in the
   range **Sharpe daily +5 to +10** with **MDD/peak-equity
   approximately −25% to −40%**. Reporting +19 as forward expectation
   would have been misleading; this post-mortem explicitly recalibrates.

## What this does *not* change

1. **No stopping rule was triggered.** Daily MDD is −0.061 BTC against
   a stopping threshold of −0.365 BTC (5.9× of remaining margin).
2. **No methodology change.** The operational rules active during this
   event (entry filters, top-K cap, ranking heuristic, expiry-guard
   handling, alpha-exit threshold) remain in force unchanged. A single
   adverse event is not sufficient evidence to modify a deliberately
   chosen trade-off.
3. **No invalidation of operational refinements.** Sessions 59, 60,
   and 61 (see `DISCLOSURES.md`) documented overrides motivated by
   live PT evidence at the time. They remain motivated. This event
   is the realization of the trade-off they accepted, not a refutation
   of the analysis.
4. **No retroactive modification.** All prior weekly reports stand.
   Trades in `events.jsonl` are unchanged.

## Update — 2026-05-07: Resolution of root cause

**Cluster extension and decision revision.** The original PM-001 (2026-05-02)
documented a single −0.063 BTC batch. Between 2026-05-04 and 2026-05-06 the
adverse pattern continued; cumulative cluster impact reached **−0.272 BTC**
over 5 consecutive trading days, with daily-horizon MDD/peak-equity touching
−101% (equity curve crossed zero on a daily-only basis).

The original "no methodology change" decision (one event, high inference
variance) was revised after the cluster materialized and quantitative
counterfactual analysis was completed.

### Counterfactual analysis (defensible replay)

Window: 2026-04-22 (operational refinements activation) through 2026-05-07
(n=111 closed daily-horizon trades). For each of the 28/29 trades that exited
via the scheduled-expiration handling path, the closing premium that would
have applied under the **canonical** (pre-refinement) early-close rule —
specifically, the median trade price observed within a ±12-hour window around
three days before each contract's scheduled expiration — was retrieved from
the public Deribit trade tape via `options_trades` (coverage 28/29, 97%).
Counterfactual P&L: `entry_premium − replay_close_premium − fees − half-spread`.
Closing-cost proxy: actual fees and execution-side adjustment from the
real trade exit.

Result, expressed as preserved P&L in BTC over the 16-day window:

| Configuration | Preserved P&L (BTC) | Δ vs actual |
|---|---:|---:|
| Actual (refinement active) | −0.0950 | 0 |
| Refinement reverted (replay using earlier close) | **+0.2280** | **+0.323** |

Distribution of the recovery across the 28 replayed trades:

- 7 trades that closed as losses would have closed positive
- 20 trades that closed as losses would have closed as smaller losses
  (this group accounts for **74% of the total recovery**)
- 1 trade that closed as a small positive would have closed as a small loss

The recovery is not driven by eliminating losses (29 → 22 losing trades only),
but by **transforming large losses into small losses**. Top-5 trades account
for 34% of the recovery; top-10 account for 59%; top-15 account for 80%.
Distributed concentration, not outlier-driven.

### Structural diagnosis

Return profile of the early-close-disabled refinement during this window:

| Regime | Frequency | Refinement contribution vs canonical | Magnitude |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Bear/sideways stable | ~80% of days | +0.04 BTC (avoided unnecessary early closes) | Small |
| Mini-rally bear→bull within trade lifetime | ~20% of days | −0.30 BTC (near-ATM losses left to settle ITM) | Large |

Gain/loss ratio: approximately **7.5× unfavorable** to the refinement
during this window.

This profile is consistent with structural literature on volatility
selling:

- **Bondarenko (2014)** — short volatility programs realize the negative
  volatility risk premium as small recurring gains punctuated by large
  rare losses. Early-close discipline (canonical rule) acts as a
  synthetic tail-cut: it sacrifices part of the gamma-decay capture in
  exchange for variance reduction in the left tail.
- **Brown (2012, "Red-Blooded Risk")** — strategies that increase
  exposure to their own dominant risk factor display unfavorable convex
  payoffs (concave on the upside, convex on the downside). Disabling the
  early-close rule is operationally equivalent to "selling more tail" on
  a strategy that already has natural short-tail exposure.
- **Bakshi & Kapadia (2003)** — confirms that without delta-hedging,
  short-volatility strategies capture both VRP and skew premium combined
  (more return, more tail). Operational discipline acts on the tail
  side of the trade-off.

### Decision

**Reverted the early-close refinement** in all three horizons; the paper
trading now uses the canonical rule that was in effect prior to the
refinement (and that remains in the backtest as the canonical reference).

**Filter-based mitigation (separate item, also evaluated)**: a filter
designed to block entries near at-the-money under specific implied-volatility
regimes was considered as a complementary or alternative mitigation. The
counterfactual analysis showed that **applying it in addition to the early-
close revert produces equal or worse outcomes** — the two mechanisms target
the same population of losses; combining them is redundant and reduces
returns in normal regimes. The filter was therefore **not promoted**.

**Ranking-related refinement (separate concern)**: the moneyness-priority
ranking refinement applied independently in 2026-04-22 remains active. Its
contribution is being assessed via a pre-scheduled checkpoint at 45 days of
live operation (2026-06-06) on its own merits.

### Methodology change applied (to public report)

The exit/early-close convention used by the paper-trading runner was
realigned with the canonical rule on 2026-05-07. Future weekly and monthly
reports will reflect this change in their methodology section. Historical
reports (W01 through W03) and prior cumulative figures are **not
retroactively modified** — they represent what was in fact executed under
the prior configuration. This is consistent with the standing transparency
principle of this repo: no silent revision of past reports.

### Methodological lesson logged internally

Any operational refinement layered on top of an asymmetric short-vol
strategy must be audited not only by mean Sharpe / mean ROI but by the
**full return distribution** — left-tail conditional metrics
(CVaR, Expected Shortfall), peak-equity-based drawdown, and the
**asymmetric gain/loss ratio across regimes**. Concretely: a refinement
that improves stable-regime returns by +0.04 BTC over 16 days is not
acceptable if its rare-regime contribution is −0.30 BTC over the same
window, even if the rare regime has lower observed frequency in the
sample at hand.

This lesson, framed as a heuristic for future refinement audits, has been
recorded in the internal methodology archive and will be applied to any
future operational refinement before it enters live operation.
