# Post-Mortem PM-002 — 2026-06-08

**A ~+4% rebound that reversed before settlement consolidated. Recorded
for completeness between PM-001 and PM-003; no material loss, no
methodology change.**

---

## Summary

Between 2026-06-06 and 2026-06-08, BTC rebounded from ~60,850 to ~63,400
USD — about **+4% in two days (~2% per day)** — briefly pushing some
near-the-money daily short CALLs toward in-the-money. The move then
**reversed** (back to ~60,900 over the following two days), and the latent
in-the-money exposure decayed back into gains before the relevant
settlements consolidated. The net effect was immaterial.

This note is published only to **close the numbering** between PM-001
(2026-05-02) and PM-003 (2026-06-16). PM-002 is the case where the adverse
move did *not* persist — and that is precisely why it left no mark.

## What happened

| Date | BTC (USD) | Δ |
|---|---|---|
| Jun 6 | 60,850 | (trough) |
| Jun 7 | 63,200 | +3.9% |
| Jun 8 | 63,400 | +0.2% (peak) |
| Jun 9 | 61,600 | −2.8% |
| Jun 10 | 60,900 | −1.1% (reversal complete) |

A short, fast rebound that round-tripped within four days.

## Why it left no material mark

The velocity (~2%/day) was above the near-ATM book's break-even, so on a
trajectory that *persisted to settlement* this would have been a
PM-001-class loss. It was not — the rebound reversed. PM-002 is therefore
the **mirror image** of PM-001 (which persisted to a fast settlement) and
of PM-003 (which persisted as a slow grind): **persistence to settlement,
not magnitude, is what turns a rebound into a realized loss.**

One honest cost did register: the early-close discipline realized part of
the position near the top, and in a reversal that close is paid but not
recovered. This is the **symmetric cost** of the same rule that cuts the
tail when moves persist — a small, expected price for the asymmetry it buys
elsewhere. The deeper out-of-the-money configuration was immune throughout.

## What this confirms

- Persistence-to-settlement is the decisive variable, not the size of the
  intraday move.
- The early-close rule has a two-sided payoff: it cuts losses when moves
  persist and costs a little when they reverse. Both sides are expected.
- No stopping rule was triggered; no methodology change is made.
